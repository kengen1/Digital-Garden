### 🌿 About This Garden

- Learning is a messy and nonlinear process.
- This is my way of establishing some sense of order amidst the constant bombardment of new information and concepts.
- While this is particularly useful in my career as a software engineer, I also find it valuable for topics outside of work—helping me think deeper about things that simply interest me.

### 🚧 Philosophy Behind the Digital Garden

- Unlike a formal blog, these notes are never "finished."
- They are a constant work in progress—some might be incomplete, filled with questions, or just quick scribbles.
- This space reflects the way a gardener tends to their garden, allowing ideas to grow while pruning and refining along the way.

This post on [networked thought](https://jzhao.xyz/posts/networked-thought) really resonated with me, so feel free to give it a read.

### 🗺️ How to Navigate

- **Graph View**: Click on the graph icon to see a visual overview of how my notes are connected.
- **Search**: Use the search functionality at the top of the page to find specific topics (`Ctrl + K`).
- **Tags**: Each note may have tags to help you discover related content.
